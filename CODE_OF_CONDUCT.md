# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Atlas is built by people who care about real work. We hold each other to three things:

- **Be direct, be kind.** Say the true thing, respectfully. Critique work, not people.
- **Assume good faith.** Ask before you accuse. Most friction is a missing context, not malice.
- **Keep it honest.** Cite your sources. Don't pass off others' work as your own. Evidence over claims — here too.

## Not tolerated

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@morrio.ai**. Maintainers may remove contributions or contributors that violate this standard. Reports are handled confidentially.
