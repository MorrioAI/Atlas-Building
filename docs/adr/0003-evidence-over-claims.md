# ADR-0003: Evidence over claims

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Résumés are claims. The entire premise of Atlas is that claims are the problem.

## Decision
No score, badge, or profile assertion exists without a linkable artifact behind it (a deliverable, transcript, or human review). If we can't cite it, we don't show it.

## Consequences
Constrains what we can display, and that's the point. Trust is the product.
