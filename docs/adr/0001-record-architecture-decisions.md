# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Decisions get made in chat and lost. New contributors re-litigate settled questions.

## Decision
We record significant, hard-to-reverse decisions as short ADRs in `docs/adr`, numbered and immutable. Superseding an ADR means writing a new one that references the old.

## Consequences
A durable, greppable trail of *why*. Small overhead per decision; large savings in re-explaining.
