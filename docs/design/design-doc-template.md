# Design — Design Doc Template

Design at Atlas starts in writing, not in Figma. Pixels come after the argument holds.

## 1. The user & the moment
Who is this for, and what are they trying to do at the exact moment they hit this surface?

## 2. The job to be done
The outcome the user wants — not the feature. "Prove I can do this" beats "a profile page".

## 3. Principles at play
Which product principles constrain this? (e.g. evidence over claims, confidence is visible.)

## 4. Flows
The happy path, plus the empty, loading, error, and first-run states. Name them all.

## 5. Proposal
Wireframes or a prototype link. Annotate the decisions, not the obvious parts.

## 6. Alternatives & tradeoffs
What you tried and rejected.

## 7. Accessibility & responsiveness
Contrast, focus states, keyboard paths, small-screen behavior. Non-negotiable.

> If you can't describe the empty and error states, the design isn't done.
