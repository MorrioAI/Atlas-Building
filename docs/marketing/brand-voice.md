# Marketing — Brand & Voice

## What we sound like
Direct, evidence-first, no hype. We earned the right to a strong claim by doing the work — so we state it plainly and let the proof carry it.

- **Say the true thing.** "We score your judgment, not your output." Not "revolutionize your career."
- **Show, don't adjective.** A real Challenge transcript beats the word "powerful".
- **Respect the reader.** They're smart and busy. Lead with the point.

## Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Proof, not promises." | "Game-changing AI platform." |
| Concrete numbers, cited. | Vague superlatives. |
| Active voice, short sentences. | Jargon, hedging, exclamation stacks. |

## Mechanics
- One idea per post. The first line earns the second.
- Every external claim links to evidence.
- Titles are specific: "The end of the résumé" > "Rethinking hiring".
